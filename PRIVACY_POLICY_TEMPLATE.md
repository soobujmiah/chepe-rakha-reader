# Chepe Rakha Reader — Privacy Policy Template

**Last Updated:** September 2026

## Introduction

চੇપે રાખા ইতিহাস ("the App") is a single-book EPUB reader application developed by Sobuj Miah. This Privacy Policy explains how we handle your data.

## Information We Collect

### Automatically Collected (Local Only)
- Reading progress and position
- Bookmarks you create
- Reading preferences (theme, font size, etc.)
- App usage analytics (anonymous, via AdMob)

### Information We Do NOT Collect
- No personal identifiers
- No reading history sent to servers
- No location data
- No contact information
- No payment information (ads only)

## How We Use Information

1. **Reading Experience**: Save your progress locally so you can resume reading
2. **Advertising**: Show relevant ads through Google Mobile Ads SDK
3. **Analytics**: Understand anonymous usage patterns to improve the app

## Third-Party Services

### Google Mobile Ads SDK
- Processes advertising identifiers (AAID)
- May collect limited device information for ad targeting
- See: https://policies.google.com/privacy

### Google User Messaging Platform (UMP)
- Handles consent collection for EEA users
- Stores consent preferences locally
- See: https://developers.google.com/user-consent

## Data Storage

All your reading data (progress, bookmarks, preferences) is stored **locally on your device only**. We do not have servers that store your personal reading data.

## Your Rights

- **Access**: All your data is on your device
- **Delete**: Uninstall the app to remove all data
- **Export**: Contact us to request your data export

## Children's Privacy

This app is suitable for all ages. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children under 13.
